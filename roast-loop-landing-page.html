The Science of Degassing

How The Loop Method Works

Supermarket coffee sits on shelves for months losing essential aromatics. Roast Loop delivers coffee in its exact peak flavor window.

01

Micro-Lot Sourcing

We partner directly with high-altitude micro-farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Costa Rica during peak harvest months.

02

Tuesday Roast Batch

Our master roaster in Brooklyn roasts every batch on Tuesday mornings in Loring eco-friendly air-roasters for maximum floral extraction.

03

Nitrogen Degas Flush

Bags are immediately sealed with one-way valves & nitrogen flushed to lock in volatile aromatic compounds during transit.

04

Peak Flavor Arrival

Arrives on your doorstep between Day 3 and Day 5 post-roast — precisely when carbon dioxide degassing stabilizes for optimal brew depth.
